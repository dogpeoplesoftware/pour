Pour is a free, open-source voice dictation app. Tap to talk, and your words appear as text — fully offline, no accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions.

Everything you dictate lands in the Bucket, a quick-capture inbox. File pieces into Projects to organize longer writing. Export to plain text, Markdown, Fountain (for screenwriters), HTML, or PDF.

* On-device speech recognition (no internet needed after model download)
* Bucket inbox + Projects with folders
* 7 themes including dark mode
* Backup & restore your entire library
* Free software under GPL-3.0
